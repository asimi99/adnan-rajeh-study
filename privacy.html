Adnan Rajeh Study

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Adnan Rajeh Study is a free, independent study site run by a student. It organizes publicly available lectures by Sh. Adnan Rajeh so they are easier to study. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sh. Adnan Rajeh or any of his organizations.

What you can do without an account

Everything on the site can be read without signing in. Without an account, your progress and reflections stay only on your own device in your browser storage. We do not receive them.

What we collect if you sign in

  • Your email address and, if you use Google or Facebook, your name as provided by them. We request only your basic profile and email.
  • Your saved study data: reflections you write, lectures you have watched, and where you stopped.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, friends list, or anything else from Google or Facebook.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and to sync your study data across your devices. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Account and study data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are never visible to us.

Third parties on the site

  • Videos play through an embedded YouTube player using the privacy-enhanced domain. YouTube may set its own cookies when you play a video, under its own policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Sign in with Google and Facebook is optional. Those companies handle your login under their own policies.

Your choices

You can delete your saved study data from the My study page when signed in, or ask for your account to be deleted completely. See how to delete your data.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.

Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/asimi99/adnan-rajeh-study/issues for any privacy question or request.
