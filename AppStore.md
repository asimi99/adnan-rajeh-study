## Description

Study the teaching of Sh. Adnan Rajeh in one place.

Every khutbah, lecture series, tafseer, seerah episode, Ramadan reflection and khaterah is organised by series and by subject. Each transcribed lecture comes with an outline you can tap to jump straight to that moment, a summary of the main points, the verses he explains and the narrations he mentions.

Search every word he has said, in English or Arabic, and land on the exact moment in the video. Study a subject from start to finish with themes such as patience, marriage, purpose and justice, each drawing together every lecture where it comes up.

Keep track of your study:
- Pick up any lecture where you left off
- Write a reflection on each lecture
- Save lectures to come back to
- Daily study reminders that point to what you were last watching
- Listen to the Tazkiyah halaqah in the background and on the lock screen
- Save the whole library for offline reading

Sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or email to keep your progress in sync with the website.

## App Privacy answers

## Review notes
- The app embeds public YouTube videos with the official player. It does not download or play YouTube audio in the background.
- Background audio is used only for the Tazkiyah halaqah recordings, which are hosted by the developer.
- The Tazkiyah section is behind a group code: 0000.
- Account deletion: My study > Settings > Delete account.
