Delete my data

Last updated November 15, 2026

You can remove your data at any time.

Delete your saved study data

  • Sign in, open My study, and choose Delete my saved data. This erases your reflections and watch history from your account and from this device.

Delete your account completely

  • Open an issue at https://github.com/asimi99/adnan-rajeh-study/issues titled "Delete my account".
  • Write the email address you signed in with. Do not post anything private. We will confirm and then delete your account and all stored data.
  • If you signed in with Facebook or Google, you can also remove this app from your Facebook or Google account settings at any time.

What is deleted

Your email, your name, and all saved study data. Nothing is kept for backup purposes beyond routine provider backups that expire on their own.
